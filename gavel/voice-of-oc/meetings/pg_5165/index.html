Review of Santa Ana Police Department Response to Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) Agents 911 Call Resulting in Arrest of Two Individuals for Stalking, Interference with Law Enforcement and Conspiracy which Orange County District Attorney Declined to Prosecute

The Commission was expected to review and comment on the Santa Ana Police Department's response to a 911 call made by Immigration and Customs Enforcement agents that resulted in the arrest of two individuals for stalking, interference with law enforcement, and conspiracy charges. The Orange County District Attorney subsequently declined to prosecute the case, and the Commission was scheduled to evaluate the department's actions and response protocols in this incident.
