Discussion

155-157 Irving Avenue Project Zoning Amendment and Building Permit Timeline Extension

The Board was scheduled to conduct a public hearing on a proposed local law amendment to Chapter 345 Zoning that would provide the 155-157 Irving Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its existing Site Plan Approval from November 27, 2023. The Board was expected to consider Resolution 0.5, which would find the action to be a Qualified Action exempt from SEQRA review and adopt Local Law No. I-XX of 2026.

157 Westchester Avenue Project Zoning Amendment and Building Permit Timeline Extension

The Board was scheduled to conduct a public hearing on a proposed local law amendment to Chapter 345 Zoning that would provide the 157 Westchester Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its existing Site Plan Approval from November 27, 2023. The Board was expected to consider Resolution 0.75, which would find the action to be a Qualified Action exempt from SEQRA review and adopt Local Law No. I-XXI of 2026.

Defined Time Frame for Street Closures and Special Event Permits

The Board was scheduled to discuss setting a defined time frame for requests related to street closures, police escorts, and use of Village property. This discussion item was carried over from the October 5 meeting and was requested by the Board of Trustees.

Department of Code Enforcement Presentation

The Board was scheduled to receive a presentation from Acting Director of Code Enforcement Michael J. Panella regarding the Department of Code Enforcement's activities and operations.

Budget Transfer - Fire Department

The Board was scheduled to consider a budget transfer resolution for the Fiscal Year 2026-27 General Fund Fire Department in the amount of $4,950.

Free Parking for 2026 Holiday Season

The Board was scheduled to consider a resolution providing free parking for the Village during the 2026 Holiday Season.
