Based on the published agenda, the Board of Trustees of Port Chester was scheduled to hold a regular meeting on October 19, 2026, to address zoning amendments for two development projects, discuss e-bike and scooter safety, consider term limits and parking fee adjustments, and vote on several resolutions including traffic improvements and budget amendments. Discussion

E-bike and scooter safety in the Village

The Board was scheduled to discuss e-bike and scooter safety concerns within the Village. This discussion was requested by Mayor Marino and Trustee Dundon as an add-on agenda item.

155-157 Irving Avenue zoning amendment and SEQRA exemption

The Board was set to hold a public hearing and consider Resolution 0.5 to amend the Village zoning code to provide the 155-157 Irving Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its Site Plan Approval. The resolution was scheduled to find the action a qualified action exempt from SEQRA review and adopt Local Law No. I-XX of 2026.

157 Westchester Avenue zoning amendment and SEQRA exemption

The Board was set to hold a public hearing and consider Resolution 0.75 to amend the Village zoning code to provide the 157 Westchester Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its Site Plan Approval. The resolution was scheduled to find the action a qualified action exempt from SEQRA review and adopt Local Law No. I-XXI of 2026.

IMA with the Town of Rye regarding parking ticket collection

The Board was scheduled to receive an update on the Intergovernmental Agreement with the Town of Rye regarding outstanding parking ticket collection. This item was requested by the Board of Trustees and carried over from the October 5th meeting.

Setting term limits for the Mayor and Board of Trustees

The Board was scheduled to discuss the establishment of term limits for the Mayor and Board of Trustees. This discussion was requested as an add-on agenda item by Trustee Dorazio.

Overnight Parking and Street Cleaning Fees Adjustments

The Board was set to discuss potential adjustments to overnight parking and street cleaning fees in the Village. This discussion was requested by Trustee Dorazio.

Street sign for Linden Place

The Board was scheduled to consider approval of a street sign for Linden Place acknowledging an autistic child in the neighborhood. This discussion was requested by Trustee Dorazio.

Meals on Main Street curbside parking request

The Board was set to consider correspondence from Jon Haseltine on behalf of Meals on Main Street requesting the Village designate 3-4 curbside parking spaces directly adjacent to their location at 509-511 North Main Street.
