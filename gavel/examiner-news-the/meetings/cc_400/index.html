The board was scheduled to receive a presentation on the progress of the Port Chester Police Department headquarters masterplan from architectural firm Ronnette Riley Architects. Public hearing on 155-157 Irving Avenue zoning amendment

The board was set to hold a public hearing to consider a local law amending Chapter 345 (Zoning) to grant the 155-157 Irving Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its existing Site Plan Approval from November 27, 2023. Resolution 0.5 was scheduled for adoption, finding the action a Qualified Action under SEQRA exempt from environmental review.

Public hearing on 157 Westchester Avenue zoning amendment

The board was set to hold a public hearing to consider a local law amending Chapter 345 (Zoning) to grant the 157 Westchester Avenue project additional time to secure a building permit under its existing Site Plan Approval from November 27, 2023. Resolution 0.75 was scheduled for adoption, finding the action a Qualified Action under SEQRA exempt from environmental review.

Public hearing on parking violation fine amendments

The board was scheduled to hold a public hearing on a local law amending Chapter 319 (Vehicles and Traffic) to establish graduated fines for overnight parking and street cleaning violations and establish minimum fines for parking violations in Port Chester.

ADD-ON: License agreement with Capitol Theatre

The board was expected to consider a license agreement with the Capitol Theatre as an add-on resolution. The specific terms and details of the agreement were not indicated on the agenda.

ADD-ON: Local law on special events, parades and public gatherings

The board was scheduled to set a public hearing to consider a local law repealing Chapter 157 (circuses, carnivals and parades) and adding a new Chapter 278 (special events, parades and public gatherings), while amending Chapters 175 (fees) and 236 (parks). This consolidation was expected to modernize the village's regulations for public events.

ADD-ON: Traffic study for King Street crosswalk

The board was set to consider correspondence from the Traffic Commission recommending a traffic study for a possible crosswalk on King Street at Davenport Avenue. This study was expected to evaluate pedestrian safety at this intersection.
