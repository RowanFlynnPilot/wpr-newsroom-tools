Discussion

Tentative Tract Map No. 2026-0010 and Density Bonus Agreement No. 2026-02

The Planning Commission was scheduled to consider an application by Home Expo Commercial LLC for approval of a Tentative Tract Map and Density Bonus Agreement to facilitate construction of a 20-unit for-sale townhome development with one deed-restricted affordable unit for very low-income households on West Fifth Street within the Harbor Mixed-Use Transit Corridor Specific Plan area. The project was set to request five incentives/concessions and waivers or reductions from development standards related to common open space, private open space, and building setback under State Density Bonus Law. Environmental review was expected to rely on a 2014 Environmental Impact Report certified for the specific plan, with a Notice of Exemption filed under CEQA Guidelines Section 15195.

Action items

  • Scheduled to adopt a resolution approving Tentative Tract Map No. 2026-0010 as conditioned
  • Scheduled to adopt a resolution approving density bonus concession and waivers or reductions in development standards as memorialized in Density Bonus Agreement No. 2026-02 as conditioned
