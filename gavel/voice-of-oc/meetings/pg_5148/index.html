Discussion

Historic Resources Commission Application No. 2026-11 and Historic Register Categorization No. 2026-6 for 1101 West Riviera Drive

The Commission was scheduled to consider approval of a Historic Resources Commission Application and Historic Register Categorization to place the single-family residence property at 1101 West Riviera Drive on the Santa Ana Register of Historical Properties as a 'Contributive' property. The applicants, Esmeralda and Anthony Henry Terusa, were also seeking approval of a Historic Property Preservation Agreement with the City, with recommended action to adopt a resolution and recommend City Council authorization of a Mills Act agreement.

Conditional Use Permit No. 2026-0017 for Laundromat at 413 West Seventeenth Street, Unit 102

The Commission was set to consider a conditional use permit request to reestablish a laundromat within an existing 2,051-square-foot commercial tenant space in a multi-tenant commercial building located in the Community Commercial zoning district. The project was expected to be reviewed for compliance with municipal code requirements, with recommended action to adopt a resolution approving the permit as conditioned.

Conditional Use Permit No. 2026-0014 for Wireless Communications Facility at 2624 South Hickory Street

The Commission was scheduled to consider approval of a conditional use permit to renew entitlements for an existing 57-foot tall major wireless communications facility designed as a 'mono-pine' located in the Light Industrial zoning district. The renewal application was expected to be evaluated under CEQA exemption guidelines with recommended action to adopt a resolution approving the permit as conditioned.

Conditional Use Permit No. 2026-0016 for Wireless Communications Facility at 1200 East Glenwood Place

The Commission was set to consider a conditional use permit to renew entitlements for an existing 60-foot tall major wireless communications facility designed as a mono-eucalyptus in the Light Industrial zoning district. The renewal was expected to be reviewed with recommended action to adopt a resolution approving the permit as conditioned under CEQA exemption provisions.
