CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP) NO. 2026-0011 FOR 1029 EAST FOURTH STREET

The Planning Commission was scheduled to consider a conditional use permit application by Verizon Wireless, represented by NuWave Communications, to renew entitlements for an existing 60-foot tall major wireless communications facility screened as a mono-eucalyptus within the Transit Zoning Code/Specific Development No. 84 district. The project was determined to be exempt from further environmental review under CEQA Section 15301.

CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP) NO. 2026-0012 FOR 711 SOUTH GRAND AVENUE

The Planning Commission was set to consider a conditional use permit application by ATC Sequoia LLC, represented by Allen Matkins, to renew entitlements for an existing 58-foot tall major wireless communications facility screened as a mono-pine within the Light Industrial (M-1) zoning district. The project was determined to be exempt from further environmental review under CEQA Section 15301.

HISTORIC RESOURCES COMMISSION APPLICATION (HRCA) NO. 2026-7 FOR 2361 NORTH RIVERSIDE DRIVE

The Planning Commission was expected to consider applications to place the property at 2361 North Riverside Drive on the Santa Ana Register of Historical Properties as 'Contributive' and to execute a Historic Property Preservation Agreement (Mills Act agreement) with the property trustees. The Commission was scheduled to adopt a resolution approving the historic designation and recommend City Council authorize execution of the preservation agreement.
