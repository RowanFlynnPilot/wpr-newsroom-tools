City Council Meeting

November 17, 2026
Public SafetyAnimal ServicesSustainabilityDowntown Business DevelopmentMunicipal Governance

Louisville City Council approved its consent agenda and two ordinances on first reading during the November 17, 2026 meeting. Key approvals included agreements for animal impound services, SWAT team participation, the Revitalization Commission budget, and electric vehicle charger grants. The council also advanced two ordinances amending municipal code regarding hearing officers for licensing and boards and commissions structure, both scheduled for second reading and public hearing on December 1.

Discussion

Approval of Agenda

The agenda was approved 5-0. Moved by Deb Fahey and seconded by Barbara Hamlington. All council members voted in favor: Christopher Leh, Barbara Hamlington, Deb Fahey, Dietrich Hoefner, and Josh Cooperman.

Approval of Minutes

Minutes were approved as part of the consent agenda. Vote passed.

Resolution No. 117, Series 2026 - Agreement with Humane Society of Boulder Valley for Animal Impound Services

Resolution approving the animal impound services agreement with the Humane Society of Boulder Valley at $14,200 for 2027 and $14,700 for 2028 was approved as part of the consent agenda. Vote passed.

Resolution No. 118, Series 2026 - Amendment to Intergovernmental Agreement for Collective SWAT Team

Resolution authorizing the city's participation in a collective SWAT team with an annual payment of $1,500 per assigned officer was approved as part of the consent agenda. Vote passed.

Resolution No. 119, Series 2026 - Louisville Revitalization Commission Budget for Fiscal Year 2027

The annual budget for the Louisville Revitalization Commission for fiscal year 2027 was approved as part of the consent agenda. Vote passed.

Electric Vehicle Charger Grant Award Acceptance and Consultant Contract Award

Both Resolution No. 94 (grant acceptance from Colorado Energy Office) and Resolution No. 121 (contract with Winn-Marion Companies for EV charger installation) were approved as part of the consent agenda. Votes passed.

Discussion/Direction: Cellular Service in Louisville

Council discussed cellular service issues in Louisville. This was a discussion and direction item with no final decision made; the matter may return for action at a later date.

Discussion/Direction/Action: Resident Artist Pilot Program

Council discussed the resident artist pilot program. This was a discussion and direction item with no final decision made; the matter may return for action at a later date.

Resolution No. 120, Series 2026 - Downtown Business Association Agreement for Street Faire

Resolution approving a license, improvement and assistance agreement with the Downtown Business Association for the 2027-2028 street faire season with financial support up to $92,000 per season. Vote outcome not specified in vote records.

Ordinance No. 1939, Series 2026 - Hearing Officer for Licensing Determinations (1st Reading)

Ordinance amending the Louisville Municipal Code to authorize appointment of a hearing officer for licensing determinations passed on first reading. Second reading and public hearing scheduled for December 1, 2026.

Ordinance No. 1940, Series 2026 - Boards and Commissions Amendments (1st Reading)

Ordinance amending the Louisville Municipal Code regarding boards and commissions passed on first reading. Second reading and public hearing scheduled for December 1, 2026.
