Discussion

Case #2026-0261: 35 Traverse Avenue Parking Variance

The Board was scheduled to issue findings on an application by Ana Soto Fedigan of Omni Architecture for a variance regarding 19 parking spaces at the Spanish United Pentecostal Church located at 35 Traverse Avenue in the CD-5 Zoning District. This matter was set for a findings determination following prior proceedings.

Case #2026-0270: The Capitol Theatre Electronic Message Boards

The Board was scheduled to issue findings on an application submitted by The Capitol Theatre, LLC through counsel Anthony Gioffre, Esq. to install electronic message boards on the existing marquee sign at 149 Westchester Avenue in the CD-6T Zoning District. This matter was set for findings determination on the signage modification request.

Case #2026-0262: D&B Company Shipping Containers Interpretation

The Board was scheduled to hold a public hearing and consider a request to adjourn an application by D&B Company regarding an interpretation on whether existing shipping containers at 315 Boston Post Road constitute developments and improvements requiring approval under Village of Port Chester Zoning Code §345-402(A). The applicant sought clarification on the regulatory status of the containers in the CD-4MU Zoning District.

Case #2026-0271: Carpet World Band Signs Variances

The Board was scheduled to hold a public hearing on an application by Carpet World for variances to permit 2 additional band signs on the building frontage at 140 Midland Avenue in the CD-4MU Zoning District. The applicant sought approval for expanded signage beyond current zoning allowances.
