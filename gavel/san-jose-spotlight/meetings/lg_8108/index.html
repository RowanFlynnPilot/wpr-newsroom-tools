Discussion

Digital Inclusion Month Proclamation

The Council was scheduled to receive a presentation of a proclamation recognizing October 2026 as Digital Inclusion Month to highlight programs and support services offered by the San José Public Library, including access to SJ Access Tech Hubs, internet service, and digital skills training.

National Cat Day Proclamation

The Council was scheduled to receive a presentation of a proclamation declaring October 29, 2026, as National Cat Day to recognize the importance of cat welfare, responsible pet ownership, and the efforts of organizations dedicated to protecting and caring for cats in the community.

National Domestic Violence Awareness Month Proclamation

The Council was scheduled to receive a presentation of a proclamation declaring October 2026 as National Domestic Violence Awareness Month to uplift prevention programs and community-based and law enforcement strategies to end domestic violence in San José.

Andrea Mackenzie Commendation

The Council was scheduled to present a commendation recognizing Andrea Mackenzie for 15 years of leadership at the Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority, during which she helped protect more than 15,000 acres of habitat and open space.

ALCLEAR, LLC Concession Agreement Amendment

The Council was scheduled to consider an amendment to the concession agreement with ALCLEAR, LLC as part of the consent calendar items.

Universal Protection Security Systems Agreement Amendment

The Council was scheduled to consider an amendment to the agreement with Universal Protection Security Systems, LP for Internet Protocol Television Support and Maintenance Services.

Appropriations Ordinance for 2025-2026

The Council was scheduled to adopt an ordinance ratifying final expenditures in various appropriations for 2025-2026 and approve associated appropriation ordinance and funding sources resolution amendments.

State Fire Apparatus and Equipment Agreement

The Council was scheduled to consider an agreement with the State of California Governor's Office of Emergency Services for fire apparatus and equipment.

HomeFirst Grant Agreement for Warming Locations

The Council was scheduled to consider a grant agreement with HomeFirst Services of Santa Clara County for city-designated overnight warming locations.

Proposition 64 Public Health and Safety Grant Program

The Council was scheduled to consider actions related to the Proposition 64 Public Health and Safety Grant Program.

Elia Park Project

The Council was scheduled to consider actions related to the Elia Park Project (10242 - PARK).

Vehicle Abatement Audit Report

The Council was scheduled to review the Vehicle Abatement Audit Report as a strategic support item.

Municipal Employees' Federation Labor Agreement

The Council was scheduled to approve the terms of an agreement with the Municipal Employees' Federation, AFSCME, Local 101 (MEF) for the term of July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2029.

Association of Engineers and Architects Labor Agreement

The Council was scheduled to approve the terms of an agreement with the Association of Engineers and Architects, IFPTE, Local 21 and related associations for the term of July 1, 2026, through June 30, 2029.

Solid Waste Management Code Amendments

The Council was scheduled to consider amendments to Chapter 9.10 (Solid Waste Management) of Title 9 of the San José Municipal Code.

Potable Water Irrigation Amendment

The Council was scheduled to consider an amendment to Chapter 15.10 of Title 15 of the San José Municipal Code to implement California Water Code Section 10608.14 regarding the use of potable water to irrigate nonfunctional turf.

Plaza de César Chávez Park Renaming

The Council was scheduled to consider the renaming of Plaza de César Chávez Park.

Parkmoor Community Apartments Permanent Financing

The Council was scheduled to consider the conversion of Parkmoor Community Apartments to permanent financing.
