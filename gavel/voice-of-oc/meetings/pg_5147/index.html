Discussion

Conference with Legal Counsel – Existing Litigation

The City Council was scheduled to discuss in closed session three existing litigation matters including Beatrice Hightower, et al. v. City of Santa Ana, et al. (federal court case), Santa Ana Police Officers Association v. City of Santa Ana (PERB case), and In re Aqueous Film-Forming Foam Products Liability Litigation (federal MDL). Discussion was set to occur outside the public view pursuant to the Brown Act due to potential prejudice to the City's litigation position.

Appoint Joese Hernandez to Community Development Commission

The City Council was scheduled to consider appointing Joese Hernandez, nominated by Councilmember Vazquez, as the Ward 2 Representative to the Community Development Commission for a partial term expiring December 8, 2026. The appointment was expected to include administering the Oath of Office to the appointee.

Report of Settlements for General Liability and Worker's Compensation Claims

The City Council was scheduled to receive a report on settlements authorized by the City Manager, City Attorney, and Human Resources Director for general liability claims up to $50,000 and worker's compensation claims up to $150,000. This report was set to be submitted pursuant to City Council Resolution 2018-045.
