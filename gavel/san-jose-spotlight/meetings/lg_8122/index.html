Native American Heritage Month Proclamation

The Council was scheduled to receive a proclamation declaring November 2026 as Native American Heritage Month, to be presented to the Muwekma Ohlone Tribe in recognition of Native American history, culture, and contributions to San José and the nation.

Diwali Festival of Lights Proclamation

The Council was set to consider a proclamation declaring November 8, 2026, as Diwali, the Festival of Lights, celebrating the holiday's traditions, cultural significance, and message of compassion, harmony, and inclusiveness in the City of San José.

Alzheimer's Disease Awareness Month Proclamation

The Council was expected to adopt a proclamation declaring November 2026 as Alzheimer's Disease Awareness Month in honor of the 'Light the World in Teal' Global Alzheimer's Awareness Initiative to raise awareness and show support for those affected by Alzheimer's disease.

Interim Ordinance for Temporary Moratorium on Detention and Custodial Facilities

The Council was scheduled to consider an interim ordinance establishing a temporary moratorium on detention and custodial facilities in San José as part of the consent calendar.

Settlement of Kao, et al v. City of San José, et al

The Council was set to review and take action on the settlement of litigation in the case of Kao, et al v. City of San José, et al, as presented on the consent calendar.

Labor Negotiations Update

The City Manager and Council were scheduled to receive an update on labor negotiations during a closed session at 9:30 a.m., with the discussion continuing into the regular session at 1:30 p.m.

Acceptance of Retirement Plans' Comprehensive Annual Investment Fee Reports for Calendar Year 2025

The Council was expected to accept the retirement plans' comprehensive annual investment fee reports for calendar year 2025, reviewing investment performance and fee structures.

Boards and Commissions Appointments

The Council was scheduled to make multiple appointments to various boards and commissions, including retirement board public member interviews and deferred compensation advisory committee appointments.

First Amendment to Master Consultant Agreement with SCI Consulting Group

The Council was set to approve a first amendment to the existing master consultant agreement with SCI Consulting Group on the consent calendar.

Torch Relay Agreement with Los Angeles Organizing Committee for the Olympic and Paralympic Games 2028

The Council was expected to consider a torch relay agreement with the Los Angeles Organizing Committee for the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games as part of consent calendar proceedings.
