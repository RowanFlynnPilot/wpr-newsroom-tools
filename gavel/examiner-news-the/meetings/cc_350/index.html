Discussion

Case #2024-0255 - 35 Traverse Avenue

The Commission was scheduled to conduct a public hearing on an application submitted by Ana Soto Fedigan on behalf of Spanish United Pentecostal Church for modification of the existing building at 35 Traverse Avenue with the addition of a new two-story rear extension. The property is located in Section 142.39, Block 1, Lot 38.

Case #2026-0275 - 21 Grace Church Street

The Commission was set to hold a public hearing on an application submitted by Aldo Vitagliano Esq. on behalf of Coffee Holding Company Inc. for Site Plan approval and Special Exception Use Approval regarding a change of use in one tenant space at 21 Grace Church Street. The application involves conversion of a space that currently houses coffee manufacturing with a marble and stone processing subtenant.

Case #2026-0274 - 179 Rectory Street

The Commission was scheduled to conduct a public hearing on an application submitted by The Sonora Group and Rafael Palomino for the creation of an outdoor dining area adjacent to an existing restaurant at 179 Rectory Street. The property is located in Section 136.71, Block 1, Lot 50.

Case #2026-0276 - 1 Westchester Avenue

The Commission was set to consider a request to adjourn on an application submitted by Nick Tose, Director of Real Estate for Costco Wholesale Corporation, for the expansion of the southeast corner of the building and addition of exterior stairs at 1 Westchester Avenue. The property is located in Section 142.31, Block 1, Lot 1.
