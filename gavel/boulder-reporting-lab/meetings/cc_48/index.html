Cellular Service in Louisville

Council was scheduled to receive information and provide direction to staff regarding cellular service availability in Louisville. The session was set to include one round of public comment, with no final decisions expected at this meeting and the potential for the item to return at a later date for action.

Resident Artist Pilot Program

Council was scheduled to discuss and consider direction regarding a resident artist pilot program. The session was set to include staff presentation, council questions, and one round of public comment, with no final action expected and the possibility of the item returning for future action.

Resolution No. 120, Series 2026 - Downtown Business Association Street Faire Agreement

Council was scheduled to consider approval of a license, improvement and assistance agreement with the Downtown Business Association for the 2027-2028 street faire season, with financial support of up to $92,000 per season. Final action was expected to be taken on this item following staff presentation and one round of public comment.

Ordinance No. 1939, Series 2026 - Hearing Officer Appointment Authority

Council was scheduled to hold the first reading of an ordinance amending the Louisville Municipal Code to authorize appointment of a hearing officer for licensing determinations. The first reading was set to include staff presentation and council questions, with no public comment opportunity and a public hearing scheduled for a later date with two rounds of public comment.

Ordinance No. 1940, Series 2026 - Boards and Commissions Amendment

Council was scheduled to hold the first reading of an ordinance amending the Louisville Municipal Code regarding boards and commissions. The first reading was set to include staff presentation and council questions, with no public comment opportunity and a public hearing scheduled for a later date with two rounds of public comment.

Resolution No. 117, Series 2026 - Animal Impound Services Agreement

Council was scheduled to consider approval of an agreement with the Humane Society of Boulder Valley for animal impound services at amounts of $14,200 for 2027 and $14,700 for 2028. This item was set for the consent agenda unless a council member requested separate consideration.

Resolution No. 118, Series 2026 - Collective SWAT Team Agreement Amendment

Council was scheduled to approve an amendment to the intergovernmental agreement establishing a collective special weapons and tactics team and authorize an annual payment of $1,500 per assigned officer. This item was set for the consent agenda unless a council member requested separate consideration.

Resolution No. 119, Series 2026 - Louisville Revitalization Commission Budget

Council was scheduled to approve the annual budget for the Louisville Revitalization Commission for fiscal year 2027. This item was set for the consent agenda unless a council member requested separate consideration.

Electric Vehicle Charger Grant Award Acceptance

Council was scheduled to accept grants from the Colorado Energy Office for EV charger installation and approve an agreement with Winn-Marion Companies for the EV charger station project. These items were set for the consent agenda unless a council member requested separate consideration.
