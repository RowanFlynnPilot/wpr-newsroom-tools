October 20, 2026

Louisville City Council approved all consent agenda items and regular business on October 20, 2026, including economic development agreements totaling $489,514 for Arsenal Health LLC and Infleqtion Quantum LLC, a $100,000 wildfire mitigation agreement with Boulder Watershed Collective, and preservation grants for the Grain Elevator and Mudrock House. The council also held a public hearing on the 2027-2028 budget presentation.
