Gavel

Keeps watch on local government meetings so your reporters don't have to sit through all of them.

Grew out of the meeting tracker WP&R uses to cover Marathon County government

  • Pulls agendas, minutes and packets from the meeting portals cities and counties already use, such as CivicClerk, Legistar, PrimeGov, CivicPlus AgendaCenter and BoardBook.
  • Transcribes meeting video posted to YouTube.
  • Summarizes each meeting item by item, with the votes where the portal records them, so you can decide what to cover and what to skip.

We set it up for the boards you cover and run it for you. There's nothing to install.

  • $75a monthOne board. The council or the school board, item by item, with the votes where the portal posts them.
  • $150a monthThe beat. Every board in one city, from the council to the plan commission, schools and utilities.
  • $350a monthThe county. Every municipality and district in your area that Gavel can read, up to eight.

Revenue Desk

A whole ad-sales system for a newsroom with one salesperson, or none.

Ad Watch has run on Wausau stations since late August, every week

  • Ad Watch records local radio during drive time, transcribes the ads and sends a weekly list of businesses already paying to advertise: warm leads.
  • Sales Desk keeps every contact, follow-up and package in one place.
  • Selling guides by category (banks, law firms, auto dealers, home services) with pitch mock-ups of real ads on your pages.
  • A results report for each sponsor after a run, for renewals.
$125a month. If your first 90 days don't bring at least one new advertiser, a business that hasn't bought from you in the past year, the next 90 are free.

Election Live

A live results page for election night, built from your county clerk's own reports.

Ran live for WP&R's April 2026 and August 2026 elections

  • Pulls the county's results reports as they're posted and updates through the night.
  • Checks that every race's totals add up before anything publishes.
  • Race-by-race results with precincts reporting, ward detail and a map of who's leading by municipality, all labeled unofficial.
  • Embeds on your site with one block and resizes itself as results come in.

We set it up for your county and run it on election night. Try the demo with sample results.

$400to set up your county, first election night included, then $250 a night. Or $750 for every 2027 election night. We're taking a few newsrooms for spring 2027.

Data tools

Trackers and archives built on public records, for the beats you want to own.

Built for WP&R's coverage of Wisconsin and Marathon County

Four of WP&R's ledgers are shown here, each live and updated from public records. If you have a record set you've wanted to turn into a tracker, we can build it for your coverage area.

From $2,000for a state edition of one of our ledgers, plus $50 a month to keep it current. Other trackers quoted per project.

Contest engine

Readers' choice ballots and reader photo contests that sponsors pay for, built for WP&R's own reader contests. WP&R's first contest on it, a pet contest, is planned for November, and we're opening it to a handful of newsrooms for their 2027 seasons, priced per contest.

Jobs board

A paid local job board, built for WP&R's own site as Now Hiring in Marathon County. An employer posts a job for 30 days through a checkout page (WP&R charges $75, or $150 to be featured at the top), the newsroom reviews each posting before it goes live, and the board embeds on your site with one block. WP&R's board isn't open to employers yet. Once it is, we'll set it up for other newsrooms at $75 a month, with the posting revenue staying with you.

For networks and funders

If you fund or support several local newsrooms, one agreement can cover a group of them. We set up Gavel on the boards each newsroom covers and Election Live for its county, and we run both. Each newsroom's price is the one on the rate card.
